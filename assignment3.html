Ajeenkya DY Patil University

Ajeenkya D Y Patil University (ADYPU) is a university located in Pune, Maharashtra.It focuses on innovation, creativity, and industry-oriented education. The university offers courses in engineering, management, design, law, architecture, and other fields. ADYPU provides students with opportunities for research, projects, clubs, and extracurricular activities. Its goal is to prepare students with skills for the changing world and future careers.
